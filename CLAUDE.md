# Claude guidance

This is a two-project Astryon monorepo. Follow the repository-wide instructions in [`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md) first.

For application work, also read [`app/AGENTS.md`](./app/AGENTS.md). For marketing-site work, read [`web/AGENTS.md`](./web/AGENTS.md).

Production deployment instructions are in [`DEPLOYMENT_HANDOFF.md`](./DEPLOYMENT_HANDOFF.md), and deployment review findings are recorded in [`REVIEW_NOTES.md`](./REVIEW_NOTES.md).

Keep the two projects independently deployable from Vercel with Root Directories `app` and `web`. Do not commit secrets or local environment files.
